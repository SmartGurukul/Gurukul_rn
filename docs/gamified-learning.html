Games

Points for learning, a contest for your house

पढ़ाई पर पॉइंट, हाउस के लिए मुक़ाबला।

XP, streaks, House Wars and quiz battles give students a reason to open the app every day. The points come from showing up and winning quizzes.

Book a free demo

In short: every student in Smart Gurukul has one XP score. It goes up with each day at school and each quiz win in the Arena or a Battle Room. Coming to school builds streaks, XP decides weekly leagues, and in school-wide House Wars each house’s XP is added to the house points teachers award. Students also play live Battle Room quizzes and 1v1 Arena challenges built from their teachers’ own questions.

The Game Hub

How do students earn XP?

One XP score, earned two ways. XP is the one currency behind leagues and House Wars, so it all feels like one game rather than five separate features.

  • Attendance: each day present earns XP and grows the streak.
  • Quiz wins: Arena and Battle Room wins earn XP.
  • House points: teachers can award house points for effort in class. They count for the student’s house in House Wars, not as XP.

Game modes

What game modes are there?

🔥

Streaks

Come to school, keep the streak going. The longest streak shows on each student’s Game Hub.

🏆

Weekly leagues

Students compete in league tiers from Bronze up to Gurukul Master, moving up or down each week.

🛡️

🎓

Practice

Solo practice with no opponent, to prepare before a battle.

Why games help in a school app

Most school apps are opened by the office and ignored by students. When coming to school and winning quizzes turn into XP, students have their own reason to open the app, and each time they do, they also see their attendance, results and school notices.

It rewards what schools already value. A streak is built by coming to school, and a league rank by effort across the week. House Wars turn individual effort into team spirit, familiar to every Indian school with a house system.

What teachers control

  • The questions: Battle Room and Arena quizzes use questions teachers write for their own subjects and classes. Quiz battles start once teachers have added questions.
  • Recognition: teachers can award house points for effort in class, with a reason that shows in the House Wars feed.
  • Houses: the school sets up its own houses and names.

Teachers can also draft questions with the AI question paper generator and add the multiple-choice ones after checking them.

FAQ

Gamification: common questions

What is gamified learning in a school app?

It means adding game ideas such as points, streaks, leagues and contests to school activities. In Smart Gurukul, students earn XP for coming to school and winning quizzes, and use it to climb leagues and win House Wars, where teachers’ house points count too.

Does it distract students from studies?

The game rewards school behaviour: coming to school and answering subject quizzes written by their teachers. Playing it means doing more of what school already asks.

Who writes the quiz questions?

Teachers. Battle Room and Arena quizzes use questions teachers write for their own subject and class, and they can generate questions with AI and review them first.

Can we use our school’s own houses?

Yes. The school sets up its own houses, and student XP plus teacher awards feed the House Wars standings.
